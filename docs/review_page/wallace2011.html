telluric-corrected = observed ÷ model × stellar model, blanked where the modelled transmission falls below 0.15. Compare it with the stellar model: what is left is the solar model’s own error.
the authors’ columns are the transmission they divided by — borrowed from the 1983-06-26 disc-centre pair, not fitted to this spectrum — and their corrected flux. They corrected only where that transmission is above about 0.8: below it their corrected flux is zero, and across the 0.94 µm band and the A-band it is absent, both drawn as gaps. Their corrected flux is also drawn only where our transmission clears 0.15, and is scaled by one number per window to meet ours (stated in the readout). Effective T and the authors’ transmission share the right-hand axis.
residual / pixel noise — the shaded band is ±1σ. The noise is 0.02–0.03% of continuum, so the ~1–2% the solar model misses reads as 50–100σ: judge a window by its rms in % and by the flatness of the corrected spectrum, not by σ.
